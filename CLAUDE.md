# Writing style (all site copy)

Copy across the site (intro, case studies, about) follows Keanan's voice:

- **First person, storytelling.** Write as "I", tell it as a story: what I ran into,
  what I decided, what happened. Not a spec sheet or a feature list.
- **No em dashes.** Use commas, periods, or restructure. (Applies to prose; code
  comments don't matter.)
- **No hyphens as punctuation.** Compound adjectives (well-known, real-time) are fine.
- **No corporate phrasing** (Additionally, Furthermore, It's worth noting) and no
  hedging/self-deprecation (just wanted to, sorry, I may be wrong but).
- **Shorter than the first draft wants to be.** Cut restated sentences.
- **Casual and direct, ends on something specific.** Reads like I wrote it once through,
  not polished for half an hour.
- **Keep the real detail and numbers** (tool names, metrics). Concrete over vague.

When editing copy, this is the `/keanan-fy` skill's baked-in rulebook plus a first-person
storytelling bias. There is no feedback.md; if one is added it becomes authoritative.
